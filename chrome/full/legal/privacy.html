🔒 Privacy Policy for miniCycle

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

Effective Date: October 2, 2025

Policy Version: 2.6

📋 Plain-English Summary

miniCycle does not collect personal data. The app keeps your tasks, routines, settings, reminders, and cycle history stored locally on your device. The marketing pages (product and learn-more) use a simple anonymous counter for page views and button clicks, but that counter does not store IP addresses, cookies, device identifiers, browser fingerprints, location data, or visitor profiles. No third-party analytics. No advertising trackers.

The miniCycle app itself is 100% private — your task data, routines, and settings never leave your device.

If you'd rather skip the marketing pages entirely, miniCycle.app takes you straight to the app.

Our Privacy Commitment

sparkinCreations is committed to transparency in its products. miniCycle is designed as a privacy-first application where you remain in full control of your data. All routines, tasks, reminders, and settings stay stored locally on your device.

Scope — this policy covers every version of miniCycle: the web app and installable PWA at minicycle.app, the Chrome Web Store browser extensions (both the full and Lite editions), and the standalone Lite version. They all share the same privacy-first, local-only design described here. The only external service any version contacts is the feedback form provider, and only when you choose to submit feedback (see Optional Features).

The simple truth: miniCycle does not collect, store remotely, transmit, or access your routine data, task data, or personal information. Your data is stored locally on your own device and stays there. If you choose to submit feedback or contact us, we only use that information for the purpose you provided it.

What miniCycle Does Not Collect

miniCycle does not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account details or login credentials
  • Routine, task, or cycle-completion data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data from within the app
  • Device information or IP addresses (no IPs are stored in any miniCycle database)
  • Location data
  • Advertising or profiling data

Anonymous Counter on Landing Pages

The marketing pages (product and learn-more) use a simple anonymous counter to record button clicks and page views, so we can see which content engages visitors and gauge overall traffic.

What is collected:

  • The visible label of any clicked button or link (e.g., "Get Started", "View on GitHub")
  • A page-view event when a marketing page is loaded
  • A timestamp for each event, used only for aggregate trend charts
  • A running total count for each named event

What is NOT collected:

  • No cookies
  • No names, emails, or personal identifiers
  • No IP addresses are stored in our counter database
  • No browser fingerprinting
  • No referrer URL, geolocation, or device information
  • No identifier links one event to another — events cannot be traced back to a specific visitor or session
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services

Scope. This counter runs only on the marketing pages. It does not run inside the miniCycle app. The app has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking integrations of any kind. The only external service the app ever contacts is the feedback form provider (Web3Forms) — and only when you actively submit feedback (see Optional Features). Like all websites, miniCycle is delivered through a hosting provider (Netlify); standard technical request handling by the hosting provider is separate from miniCycle's own systems, and we do not use hosting request data to identify visitors, build profiles, serve ads, or power analytics.

Data Stored on Your Device

All your data stays local. miniCycle stores the following information on your device using browser localStorage:

  • Task lists and completion history
  • Cycle configurations and progress
  • Recurring task templates
  • Reminder settings
  • Theme preferences and unlock progress
  • App settings and customizations

Important: This data remains on your specific device and browser. miniCycle cannot access this information remotely, and sparkinCreations has no visibility into your private task data. If you switch browsers or devices, your data will not transfer unless you manually export and import it.

Optional Features

miniCycle may include optional features that allow you to share limited data if you choose. Examples include:

  • Browser notifications - If enabled, task names will appear in your device's notification center (managed by your operating system). This means task data becomes visible outside the app and may be seen by others with access to your device. You can enable or disable this at any time in the Reminders settings
  • Submitting feedback or bug reports - When you choose to send feedback, your comments and any optional contact information are transmitted through a third-party form service (Web3Forms) for the sole purpose of delivering your message to us. This is the only external service the app communicates with, and only when you submit feedback. No task or routine data is included.
  • Exporting routines - You control where exported .mcyc files are saved
  • Participating in beta features - Some new features may require limited data sharing to function. No beta feature will transmit task data unless you are clearly told first and explicitly opt in.

Use of these features is always voluntary. miniCycle will never transmit your task or routine data from your device unless you explicitly choose to activate one of these features. You will always be clearly informed before any data leaves your device.

Purpose of Optional Data

Any optional data you decide to share is used solely for its stated purpose:

  • Improving the app based on your feedback
  • Enabling features you've requested (like exports or sharing)
  • Troubleshooting technical issues you've reported

We will never:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling
  • Share your data beyond its stated purpose
  • Keep your data longer than necessary

Security & Technical Safeguards

miniCycle is designed to keep your task data private and secure:

  • No remote transmission - Your data never travels over the internet unless you use an optional export/sharing feature
  • No servers storing your task data - miniCycle does not maintain servers that store your routines, tasks, cycle history, reminders, or settings
  • Input sanitization - Protection against malicious code injection
  • Browser security - Data stored in standard browser security contexts
  • Secure optional features - If you choose to use features that transmit data, that data is handled securely and only for its intended purpose

Your Rights & Control

Since miniCycle does not collect or remotely access your app data, you maintain control on your own device:

  • Delete - Clear your data anytime through the app's "Factory Reset" option or by clearing browser data
  • Export - Download your data as .mcyc files whenever you want
  • Access - All your data is already accessible to you locally
  • Correct - Edit your tasks and settings directly in the app
  • Transfer - Export and import data to move between devices

Privacy Principles & Regulations

This policy is designed with the following privacy principles in mind:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - United States
  • Other applicable privacy laws and best practices

Since miniCycle doesn't collect personal data inside the app, most privacy regulations have limited applicability to our data practices. We've designed the product to align with the spirit of these standards regardless.

This does not mean every regulation applies to miniCycle in every situation, but the app is designed around privacy principles such as transparency, user control, data minimization, and clear consent.

Children's Privacy

miniCycle does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. App data stays local on the device. Since we have no access to that data, we cannot determine the age of our users. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of any web application.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will:

  • Post the change in the changelog on our product page at minicycleapp.com
  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy so the most recent revision is always visible
  • Maintain our privacy-first approach in any new features
  • Give users full control over any new data handling options
  • Never make existing features less private without your explicit opt-in

For any changes, please refer to the changelog at minicycleapp.com. The current version of this policy is always available at minicycle.app/legal/privacy.

miniCycle's policy is simple: you are always in control of your data.
