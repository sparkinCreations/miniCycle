Features

Auto-Reset Cycles

Complete your routine. It resets automatically. No manual cleanup.

Recurring Tasks

Daily, weekly, monthly, or custom schedules — built right in.

Vocabulary Themes

Unlock themed language, icons, and colors as you complete cycles.

Undo & Redo

Full state snapshots with one-tap undo. Never lose progress.

Achievements

Milestones and rewards that grow with your consistency.

Smart Reminders

Gentle nudges so your routine never misses a step.

Private by Architecture

Works Offline

Install as a PWA. No internet required. No account needed.

Share Routines

Export .mcyc files to share reusable routine templates with other people.
