What is miniCycle?

A privacy-focused routine manager that handles repeating tasks differently than traditional to-do apps.

Routines, Not Just Tasks

Most task managers treat each task as a one-off item and delete it when complete. miniCycle is different.

miniCycle organizes your tasks into routines. When you complete all your tasks, they automatically reset so you can tackle them again. Your progress is tracked by cycle count - how many times you've completed the routine.

It's perfect for daily routines, weekly goals, and recurring habits you want to build consistency with.

🔒 100% Private - All data stays on your device

Three Modes for Different Needs

Choose how your routine behaves when you complete tasks.

The Core Concept

You create routines, you complete cycles.

Complete ➔ Cycle ➔ Repeat

Auto Cycle

To-Do Mode

Features

Everything you need to build and maintain consistent routines.

🔁 Recurring Tasks

Set tasks to automatically appear on a schedule - hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, monthly, or yearly.

📅 Due Dates & Reminders

Set deadlines and get browser notifications. Overdue tasks are highlighted so nothing slips through.

📁 Multiple Routines

Create separate routines for different areas of life - work, home, fitness, personal projects.

🔍 Search, Filter & Sort

Find tasks instantly with inline search. Filter by status, priority, due date, or recurring. Sort alphabetically, by priority, or by due date.

📊 Progress & Achievements

Track cycle counts, view statistics, and earn milestone badges and achievements as you build consistency.

🎨 Vocabulary Themes

Unlock themed vocabularies as you hit milestones - Habit Tracker, Fitness, Scholar, and more. Each theme changes labels, icons, and colors.

🔄 Drag & Drop Reordering

Rearrange tasks by dragging on desktop or long-pressing on mobile. Arrow buttons provide keyboard-accessible reordering.

♿ Accessibility

Reduced motion, high contrast mode, adjustable font sizes, and full keyboard navigation. Built for everyone.

🛡️ Backup & Restore

Create full backups of your routines, restore to any point, and factory reset when needed. Your data is always recoverable.

🎯 Focus Mode

Hides distractions and highlights the next incomplete task. Great for working through routines one step at a time.

🌙 Dark Mode & Personalization

Toggle dark mode, customize colors, background patterns, and font sizes. Your preferences persist across sessions.

💾 Export & Share

Download routines as .mcyc files. Back up your data or share templates with others.

↩️ Undo & Redo

Made a mistake? Undo recent actions. Your history is preserved per routine.

📱 Works Everywhere

Responsive design for phone, tablet, or desktop. Install as a PWA for offline use.
